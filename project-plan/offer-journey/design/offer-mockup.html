🌿 Every Family is Different — Why We Begin with a Discovery Call

Rather than offering a generic, off-the-shelf package, I listen to your family’s unique story first during an introductory Discovery Call. Every child’s nervous system is unique, and no two parenting challenges are identical.

Together on our call, we will explore what feels heaviest at home and shape a truly personalized coaching roadmap — matching the exact pace, topics, and level of support that honors your child's emotional needs and restores your own inner calm.

* Note: 1:1 coaching packages and single sessions are unlocked on your account right after we meet on your Discovery Call.

Small Parent Circles

Sharing the journey alongside parents facing the exact same daily moments.

🌱 A Circle of Parents Who Understand — Shared Support Guided by Mai

Parenting can feel deeply lonely when you're in the thick of it. In these small, intimate circles (just 4 to 8 parents), we meet weekly to share openly, learn practical tools, and experience the comforting reassurance that you are not alone or failing.

Small parent circles operate completely separately from 1:1 coaching. They do not open automatically with packages. Instead, we begin with a Discovery Call to get to know your family and connect you with parents facing the very same everyday moments.

* Circles launch collaboratively once 4 parents enroll in a shared topic, choosing meeting dates that fit everyone's family routine.
Group Topics: What We Explore Together
Topic 01
Tantrums & Big Feelings

Helping your child calm high emotions through co-regulation and connection, never fear or punishment.

Topic 02
Parental Exhaustion

Restoring your nervous system calm when you feel touched out, overwhelmed, and reactive.

Topic 03
Screen Time & Tech Habits

Ending daily device battles peacefully with clear, loving, and consistent boundaries.

Topic 04
Bedtime & Sleep Struggles

Creating comforting evening rituals that ease separation anxiety and lead to peaceful, independent sleep.

Topic 05
Sibling Clashes & Jealousy

Transforming chronic rivalry into lifelong warmth, fairness, and mutual companionship.

Topic 06
Parenting as a United Team

Getting on the exact same page with your partner so neither of you feels divided or unsupported.
