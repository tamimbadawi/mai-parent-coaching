The booking page

Three doors instead of five session types

The old list (Initial, 60-min, 90-min, Family, Follow-up) was tidy, but it described session lengths, not what a parent is trying to do. We keep the same card style and change what the cards mean.

New here

Discovery Call

  1. Pick a Tuesday slot (11:00, 11:45 or 12:30)
  2. Two quick taps: what's going on, what would help
  3. See a likely package, then book the call
  4. Mai confirms the package on the call

30 min · EGP 500 · required for new families, returning clients skip it · a separate fee, not deducted from packages

Ready to work with Mai

1:1 Coaching

  1. Choose a package, or tap "help me choose"
  2. Pay once for the package (online payment at launch)
  3. Book each 60-min session on Mon or Wed (12:00 or 13:30)
  4. Dashboard shows "3 of 4 sessions left"

New families start with a Discovery Call. Sessions must be used within about 2 weeks per session.

Learn with other parents

Group Coaching

  1. Join one of Mai's topic groups, or start your own group
  2. Group opens with 4 people, closes at 8
  3. Mai sets the day and dates once it has 4 (no fixed group day)
  4. Each parent pays their own place (per person)

60-min sessions · per-person prices · about half the per-session price of 1:1

Build order

Four phases, each one usable on its own

Now

1 · Discovery Call live

  • Mai's weekly hours, Discovery Call at 30 min, "free" wording removed (built, not committed yet)
  • Stop the booking page showing made-up times on closed days
  • Fix rescheduled sessions saving at the wrong hour
  • Calendar colours (yellow Discovery, green coaching) and one clean event layout
  • Discovery pop-up: topics, the two "help me choose" taps, review
  • Admin availability as a month calendar with real dates

Discovery Call price set at EGP 500. Payment plugs in at launch.

Next

2 · Show the offer

  • Booking page left column becomes the three doors
  • Package cards with Mai's prices, plus "help me choose"
  • New families are sent to the Discovery Call first. Returning clients see the packages directly
  • Group coaching shown as "coming soon"

Display only: parents can see the offer and the price, and nothing is charged.

Later

3 · Packages & balances

  • A package balance per client: sessions bought, used, left, and "use by" date (about 2 weeks per session)
  • Booking a coaching slot uses one session from the balance
  • Until launch, Mai can add a package to a client in admin, so the whole flow can be tested
  • The "Pay" step is one clearly marked spot that PayTabs fills at launch

Built and tested without real money, with no fake "payment successful" screens.

Later

4 · Group coaching

  • Mai creates groups: topic, 4 to 8 seats, package length
  • Parents join (seat counter) or request their own group
  • At 4 people Mai sets dates; at 8 the group closes
  • 60-min sessions, each parent pays their own place

Reuses Phase 3's package balances.

At launch

Go live · PayTabs

  • PayTabs hosted payment page for the Discovery Call (EGP 500), packages and group places
  • Payment confirmed by PayTabs' server message only, never by the browser returning
  • A booking or package becomes active only after that confirmation
  • Admin Orders shows real payments

One integration covering all three, done once the site is ready to open.
